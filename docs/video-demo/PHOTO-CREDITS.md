# Philippine hospital photographs

Only photographs of the Philippine General Hospital, Manila, are used in the opening. These are archive photographs, not footage recorded for this demo. Overseas stock clips considered during research were rejected and are not part of the edit.

- `pgh.jpg`: **UpPgh.JPG**, Ramon FVelasquez, CC0. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UpPgh.JPG). Cropped and panned; also used in the closing aperture of the opening logo transition.
- `pgh-entrance.jpg`: **Entrance to the Philippine General Hospital.JPG**, Zenji89, public domain. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Entrance_to_the_Philippine_General_Hospital.JPG). Cropped and panned.
- `pgh-cancer.jpg`: **PGH Cancer Institute Building.jpg**, Joven Jacolbia, 16 December 2022. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PGH_Cancer_Institute_Building.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Cropped, panned and darkened behind titles. The adapted photographic material remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `pgh-ward.jpg`: **Philippine General Hospital COVID-19 ward.jpg**, Department of Public Works and Highways, 28 March 2020. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Philippine_General_Hospital_COVID-19_ward.jpg). Philippine government public-domain work. Cropped and panned. Its onscreen credit identifies the March 2020 archive date; do not present it as a current ward view.
- `pgh-detail.jpg`: **PGH Pediment.JPG**, Ramiltibayan, 7 November 2014. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PGH_Pediment.JPG), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Cropped, panned and darkened behind titles. The adapted photographic material remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The edit uses six three-second shots, returning to the first photograph for visual continuity into the approved logo transition. Registration, medical records and payment are narrative headings; the images are hospital context, not claims about the function of a depicted desk or room. No endorsement by these photographers or the hospital is implied.

For publication, include the attribution and license links above with the video description. Retain this document and the relevant image assets with the editable source.
